Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated · November 16, 2026 fewori.com/refund

1. Free trial

Every new workspace starts with a 7-day free trial. A card is required to begin so the workspace keeps running after the trial, but nothing is charged during the seven days. Cancel any time before the trial ends from your billing settings and you pay nothing.

2. Plans and billing cycle

Fewori is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription; plans and prices are listed on our pricing page. Paid plans renew automatically at the end of each period until cancelled. Yearly plans are billed once, up front, for the whole period.

3. Cancellation

  • You can cancel at any time from your dashboard, without writing to us.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • After it ends, paid features — publishing, AI generation, checkout — switch off; your pages, orders and customer records stay in your workspace.
  • We do not refund the remaining days of a cancelled period.

4. Refunds

Within 14 days of your first payment

If you are new to a paid plan and the product is not what you expected, ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment and we will process it. This applies to the first subscription only, not to renewals, upgrades or additional workspaces.

After the first 14 days

Refunds are not granted automatically. We do review requests case by case when the cause is on our side, for example:

  • a prolonged outage that stopped you from using the platform;
  • a billing error — a duplicate charge or a wrong amount;
  • a change to the product that materially reduces what your plan includes.

What is not refundable

  • AI generations already delivered and used (pages created, photographs generated);
  • months already served, or the remaining days of a cancelled period;
  • the price difference when you move to a cheaper plan;
  • domain names, print or third-party services bought on your behalf.

5. Refunds for your own shoppers

Sales made on pages you publish are yours: you are the merchant, and refunds to your shoppers are issued by you through your payment provider. Fewori does not hold the money and cannot refund it for you. Our Terms of Service describe that relationship in section 5.

6. How to request a refund

Write to us through the contact section of our website with:

  • the email address on your workspace;
  • the date and amount of the charge;
  • a sentence on why you are asking.

We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

7. Plan changes

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged pro rata for the rest of the period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next period.

8. Billing disputes

If something looks wrong on a charge, write to us before opening a chargeback with your bank. We can usually fix it the same week, and a chargeback filed without contacting us first may pause your workspace while it is investigated.

9. Contact

Any question about a trial, an invoice or a cancellation: reach us through the contact section of our website.

This policy is written for the Fewori platform as described on our site. It is not legal advice; have your own counsel review it before you launch commercially.
