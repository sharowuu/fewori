Privacy Policy

Last updated · November 16, 2026 fewori.com/privacy

1. Who we are

Fewori (“Fewori”, “we”, “our”, “us”) provides a platform that turns a product photo into a finished product page: AI-written copy, AI-generated photography, publishing on your own domain, and the order, payment and contact tools that go with it (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what we collect when you use the Service, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create a workspace we collect your name, email address and password, plus the workspace and domain details you connect. Paid subscriptions are processed by our payment providers — we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Content you provide

You upload product photos, names, descriptions, prices, variants and page copy. We process this content to generate the page, the photographs and the text that you publish. Your content stays in your workspace and is never shown to other customers.

Customer and order data

When a shopper buys from a page you published, we store the details needed to fulfil the order — typically name, email, shipping address, phone, items, amounts and order status — and show them in your orders dashboard and CRM. This data belongs to your workspace: you decide who inside your team can see it.

Usage data

We record how the Service is used (pages opened, features used, generation runs, page views and form submissions) so we can keep the product working, show you performance numbers and fix problems. Usage records are tied to your workspace, not to individual shoppers.

Payment data

Shopper payments are handled by the payment provider you connect (for example Stripe or PayPal). Those providers process the card or wallet details directly; we receive the outcome of the transaction and the order record, not the payment credentials.

3. How we use information

  • to run the Service: generate pages, photographs and copy, publish them, and take orders;
  • to process subscriptions, invoices and payments;
  • to show you traffic, conversion and order numbers for your pages;
  • to send essential messages about your account, an order or a policy change;
  • to give support and answer your requests;
  • to detect abuse, fraud and technical incidents, and to keep the Service secure.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your content or your shoppers’ data to advertise anything.

4. AI processing

Page copy, product photography and some page structure are produced by AI models. To do that, the product photos, names and descriptions you provide are sent to the model provider that powers the feature. We send only what the generation needs — not your account credentials, your shoppers’ data or your other pages. Generated results are yours to edit, replace or discard before publishing.

5. Sharing with service providers

We work with a small number of providers who process data on our behalf under contract:

  • Hosting and storage — to keep the site, your pages and your uploads online;
  • Payment providers — to take subscription payments and, when you connect an account, shopper payments;
  • AI providers — to generate copy and product photography;
  • Email delivery — to send order notifications and account messages.

These providers may only use the data to deliver the service we asked for. We do not sell or rent data to data brokers.

6. Security

Connections are encrypted in transit, credentials are hashed, access to production data is limited to staff who need it, and workspace data is separated so one customer cannot read another’s. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep backups and an incident process, and we will tell you without delay if a breach affects your workspace.

7. Retention

We keep workspace data while your account is active. Order and customer records stay available to you until you delete them or close the workspace. When you close your account we delete or anonymise your content within 30 days, except for records we must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to restrict or object to how we process it. Shoppers who bought from your page should contact you first, since you are the merchant for that sale; we will help you respond.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your workspace settings. Published pages use the minimum needed to record a page view and a form submission. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not run third-party ad pixels on our own site.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. When a change is material we will post it here, update the date above, and notify workspace owners by email.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy, a data request, or a deletion: write to us through the contact section of our website and we will respond within a reasonable time.

This policy is written for the Fewori platform as described on our site. It is not legal advice; have your own counsel review it before you launch commercially.
